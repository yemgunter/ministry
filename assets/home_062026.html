About the Ministry

Igniting Intercession

Yolanda Gunter is a prophetic voice, prayer strategist, and kingdom builder committed to equipping believers for effective intercession. The 5 AM Fire Prayer Focus is a monthly gathering of faith — uniting men and women of the body of Christ across time zones in targeted, scripture-based prayer.

This June, the focus is Recover, Produce & Build — a prophetic mandate for every kingdom builder to reclaim lost ground, operate in divine productivity, and construct legacies that advance the kingdom. Whether you are leading a business, a home, a ministry, or a movement — this word is for you.
