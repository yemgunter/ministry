About the Ministry

Igniting Intercession

Yolanda Gunter is a prophetic voice, prayer strategist, and kingdom builder committed to equipping believers for effective intercession. The 5 AM Prayer Focus is a weekday morning gathering of faith — uniting men and women of the body of Christ across time zones in targeted, scripture-based prayer.

This year's focus, 2026: Revelation & Restoration, calls the body of Christ into seasons of divine unveiling and complete restoration — of vision, productivity, and legacy. Like the ant, we are called to diligence and preparation: gathering wisely, laboring with purpose, and building what will outlast us, so every dormant assignment is revived and every delayed promise comes into full manifestation.
